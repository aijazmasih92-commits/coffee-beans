01 — Our story

A cup of California,
eight thousand miles
from home.

In 1963, Herbert B. Hyman opened a small coffee house in Los Angeles with a conviction that still runs through every cup we pour: coffee and tea deserve the same care as fine wine.

He travelled to the source. He learned the harvests. He built a house around growers, not shelves — around patience, not speed. Sixty years later, that house has a Pakistani address. The recipes are unchanged. The patience is unchanged. Only the sky outside the window is different.

“Great coffee isn’t served. It’s grown, gathered, roasted — and then, finally, shared.”

02 — Heritage

Three moments
that made the cup.

A coffee house, an invention, and a homecoming — the short version of a very long love story.

  1. 1963

    The first pour

    Herbert B. Hyman opens The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf in Los Angeles, California. A small café with a very long memory.

  2. 1987

    The Ice Blended is invented

    A bartender at the café blends coffee, ice and chocolate into something the world had never tasted. The Original Ice Blended is born — and iced coffee is never the same.

  3. 2017

    Pakistan, meet California

    The first Pakistani café opens its doors. Lahore gets a new ritual; the country gets a new kind of coffee house.

  4. Today

    Still steeping

    More than twenty cafés across seven cities — and a menu that still begins at the source.
